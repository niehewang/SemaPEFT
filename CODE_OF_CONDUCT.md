# Code of Conduct

We want SemaPEFT discussions and contributions to remain professional, technically rigorous, and respectful.

Contributors should:
- discuss evidence, implementations, and claims without personal attacks;
- distinguish reproducible findings from speculation;
- respect privacy, confidentiality, and third-party licensing;
- avoid discriminatory, harassing, or threatening language;
- disclose conflicts of interest that materially affect technical evaluation.

Repository maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to keep the project safe and constructive.
